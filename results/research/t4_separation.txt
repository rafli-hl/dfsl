STEP 3 -- does tier 1's ceiling have to shrink with the horizon?
==================================================================================================
  H_T4': tier 1's ceiling is P*/sup_t B_t and B_t is degree-1, so the ceiling
  inherits the growth of an EXTREME order statistic of ||g_t||. Tier 3's B is a
  constant, so its ceiling is horizon-independent. Measured on prefixes of ONE
  fixed stream, so nothing varies but the horizon.

          T     sup||g||   ratio vs prev
      12500      581.238                
      25000      581.238           1.000
      50000      646.505           1.112
     100000      683.401           1.057
     200000     1149.161           1.682

  fitted growth exponent of sup||g|| in T: 0.220
  predicted from the committed Hill index alpha=2.43: 1/alpha = 0.412
  a doubling of T multiplies sup||g|| by 2^0.220 = 1.165  (predicted 1.330)

  So tier 1's ceiling decays with the horizon while tier 3's does not. The effect
  is REAL but SLOW -- a factor of ~1.3 per doubling, not the 5x seen between the two
  artifacts in step 2, which therefore cannot be attributed to horizon alone.
  Reported that way rather than claimed as a confirmation.
