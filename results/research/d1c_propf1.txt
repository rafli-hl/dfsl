### S3 -- the coarsening defence, costed rather than asserted
  tab:beta: the B=10^4 block tracker holds the lower bracket at 0.93, not 0.
    bracket 0.93 -> 7% of rounds violate; charged trivially
      at O(D sup sigma) per round that is 0.07*T = 14,000 rounds at T=200,000 -- Theta(T), the same wall.
    bracket 0.97 -> 3% of rounds violate; charged trivially
      at O(D sup sigma) per round that is 0.03*T = 6,000 rounds at T=200,000 -- Theta(T), the same wall.
    bracket 0.99 -> 1% of rounds violate; charged trivially
      at O(D sup sigma) per round that is 0.01*T = 2,000 rounds at T=200,000 -- Theta(T), the same wall.
  So coarsening does not escape: it trades the O(NW) lapse set for an O(T) violation
  set. The binary claim 'a coarse tracker fails the bracket' was overstated; the
  accounting reaches the same conclusion and is checkable.
